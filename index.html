Watch safety Tips

  • Use the right wrist strap: Ensure the strap is not too tight or too loose to avoid damage to the lugs or breakage of the strap.
  • Store your watch properly:
  • Use a soft cloth and mild soap or a specialized watch cleaner to wipe down your watch. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive materials. 1
  • Professional maintenance: If your watch requires repairs or maintenance, visit a professional watchmaker to assess and repair any issues.
  • Be cautious in public: Avoid displaying your watch unnecessarily in high-risk areas and be discreet in public.
  • Use insurance: Consider using an insurance-approved safe for your watch when not wearing it and always wear your watch when traveling.
  • Report theft or loss to the police within 24 hours to secure your claim.
  • Document Everything: Gather proof of ownership (receipts, appraisals, photos) and incident details (written statements, witness info, police report).
  • Check Your Policy: Check Your Policy: Review coverage limits, deductibles, exclusions, and deadlines to understand your policy fully.
